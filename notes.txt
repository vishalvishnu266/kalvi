### Architecture Decisions
- ✅ **Custom Session System** - Full control, no tower-sessions
- ✅ **Sessions in Tenant DB** - True tenant isolation
- ✅ **Users in Tenant DB** - Each tenant has isolated users
- ✅ **Tenant-Scoped Routes** - All auth routes use /t/{slug}/ prefix
- ✅ **Complete Isolation** - Users, sessions, data all isolated
- ✅ **Direct SQLite Storage** - Fast, durable, simple
- ✅ **Admin Features Ready** - Can view/revoke sessions
